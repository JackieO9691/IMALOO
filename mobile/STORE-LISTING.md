## Full description
IMALOO is where your child's favourite toys come to life.

Take a photo of a real teddy, doll, pony or any toy, and watch it wake up inside bright, magical worlds: a sunny Aussie beach, a deep forest, a castle, outer space and many more. Your child picks the world, and IMALOO makes pictures, stories and short movies starring their very own toy.

WHAT CHILDREN CAN DO
• Bring their real toys to life in 27 storybook worlds
• Make their own movies and watch them on the TV
• Read storybooks made from their own adventures, with Read to me
• Play Fruit Maths, Read & Spell and sing along with 12 songs
• Learning levels for ages 1-3, 3-4 and 4-5

MADE FOR LITTLE HANDS
Big picture buttons and very little reading, so even young children can play on their own.

SAFE BY DESIGN
• Only photos of toys are allowed. Every photo is checked, and photos of people, screens or magazines are refused
• Every picture and movie is checked before your child sees it
• No ads, and nothing is shared. Each family's toys, photos and movies are private to that family
• Grown-up settings are behind a grown-ups sum: permissions, daily play-time limit and account deletion

Imaloo — where toys come to life and childhood stays innocent.

PLANS
IMALOO includes a 7-day free trial. Families can choose a plan at app.imaloo.com.au.
