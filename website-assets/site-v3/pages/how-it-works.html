🧸 How IMALOO works

Easy steps for grown-ups. Set it up once, then little ones can play on their own with big picture buttons.

👋

1. Make your family account

Go to app.imaloo.com.au and start your 7-day free trial. No card needed. Add your children's names and ages, so the games start at the right level.

📷

2. Add a toy

Tap Toys, then take two photos of a real toy: one of the front and one of the back. IMALOO checks every photo to make sure it's a toy. Photos of people, screens and magazines are refused.

✨

3. Make something magical

Tap Make and choose what to make:

  • Wake-up movie: one toy comes to life and its room turns magical.
  • Adventure picture: up to four toys together in a world.
  • Storybook adventure: in Library, then Books.
🏰

4. Pick a land

Choose one of 54 fantasy lands, from a sunny Aussie beach to outer space. Then watch your toy wake up inside it.

📺

5. Watch it on the TV

Everything you make is kept in the Library. Open a movie and send it to your TV to watch together on the big screen.

🎮

6. Play and learn

Tap Catch Party, Maths, Spell or Songs. Children pick their age (🐣 1-3, 🐥 3-4 or 🦁 4-5) and the games grow with them. Try the games here.

👪

7. The Parents page

Behind a grown-ups question, so little fingers can't get in. Here you can:

  • See your plan and how many movies and pictures are left
  • Choose who can add toys, make movies or delete things
  • Set a daily play time
  • Change or cancel your plan, or delete your account

Questions

What age is IMALOO for?

IMALOO is made for children aged about 1 to 5, with a grown-up setting things up. The games have three age levels.

Can more than one child use it?

Yes. The Two children and Family plans let each child have their own name and age. Tap "Who's playing?" to switch.

Which devices does it work on?

IMALOO works in the web browser on phones, tablets and computers. Apps for the App Store and Google Play are coming soon.
