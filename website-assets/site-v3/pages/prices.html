Questions

What counts as a movie or a picture?

Each short movie or picture IMALOO makes counts once. A long movie counts as two. Each page painted for a storybook adventure counts as one picture. Games, songs, spelling and maths never count.

How do I change or cancel my plan?

Open the Parents page in the app (behind a grown-ups question) and choose Manage plan. You can change or cancel any time.

What happens after the free trial?

New families get 7 days free with 3 movies and 20 pictures. To keep going, choose a plan on the Parents page.

Is GST included?

Yes. All prices are in Australian dollars and include GST, and your receipt shows the GST.
