🛡️ Safe, private and kind

IMALOO is made for little children, so safety is built into every part of it.

Imaloo — where toys come to life and childhood stays innocent.

Imaloo is designed for children to enjoy imaginative, age-appropriate adventures with their favourite toys. Our content rules strictly prohibit pornography, nudity, sexual content, violence, gore, and abusive or disturbing scenes.

Only photographs of toys are permitted. Photos of real people, magazine pictures, screenshots, and other non-toy images are not allowed. Attempts to bring these images to life or create sexual, violent, or harmful content are prohibited.

Our commitment is to keep Imaloo a place for playful storytelling, creativity, and childhood wonder.

How we keep children safe

Nothing scary, no weapons, and toys only ever share hugs and high-fives.

🧸

Toy photos only

Every photo is checked before it is saved. Photos of people, screens and magazines are refused and never kept.

🔍

Everything is checked

Every picture and movie is checked again before your child sees it. If something isn't right, it's thrown away.

⌨️

Children never type

Little ones choose with picture buttons, so they can't type anything into IMALOO.

🔒

Private to your family

Nobody else can see your family's toys, photos, pictures or movies.

🚫

No ads, ever

IMALOO never shows ads to children.

👪

Grown-ups in charge

Choose who can add toys, make movies or delete things, set a daily play time, and delete your account whenever you like.
